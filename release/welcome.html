FX–USB

This installs two things:

  • FX–USB, the app, in your Applications folder.
  • The FX–USB microphone, so Zoom, Meet, FaceTime and other apps can use your FX MIC.

At the end, your Mac's sound system restarts, so any sound playing stops for a second.

FX–USB is unofficial. It is a fan-made app, not made, checked or supported by teenage engineering. The microphone is built from BlackHole by Existential Audio (GPL-3.0); its license (LICENSE) is inside the driver.
